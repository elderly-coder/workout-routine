Ladders

Three movement ladders. Five rungs each. Clear the gate cleanly before you climb.

Watch the source video ↗

The whole idea

“Reps are a treadmill. Load is a ladder.”
Start at rung one

Use the easiest version you can perform with control.

Meet the gate

Hit the listed reps or time cleanly—no shortcuts.

Earn the next rung

Progress by leverage and difficulty, not endless reps.

Climb back down

If form breaks, return to the last rung you own.

Climbing rules

Don’t skip the boring rung.

Rung two is where most people quit—not rung five. It feels like rung one, only slightly harder, for weeks. That’s exactly why it matters.

01

Never skip a rung

“Nobody in a gym jumps from the 20 to the 60. You don’t get to either.”

02

Clean means clean

The gate only counts when every rep keeps the movement’s form standard.

03

Regress without ego

A broken rep means the current rung isn’t owned yet. Step down and keep building.

One piece of gear

Pull rungs two through five require a secure doorway pull-up bar. A resistance band can bridge the jump to full pull-ups.

Everything else: floor + chair
